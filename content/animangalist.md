# AniMangaList
A modern anime and manga tracking platform that lets users organize, track, and manage their watch and reading lists.

## Usage

- Visit [AniMangaList](https://aml.snehasish.xyz) to start tracking your favorite anime and manga.
- Create a personal list of anime and manga you're watching or reading.
- Manage your collection with ease using an intuitive interface.

## What it solves

- This application allows you to maintain a centralized list of all your anime and manga across different platforms.
- You can track your progress, rate titles, add notes, and organize your collection efficiently.
- Share your list with friends or embed it on your personal website, portfolio, or any platform that supports integration.

## Tech Stack

- JavaScript
- Node.js
- React
- Database (MongoDB)
- Chakra UI
